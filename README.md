# AIR-Quality-Monitoring-system-Using-IOT
The increasing levels of air pollution pose significant threats to public health and the environment, making real-time air quality monitoring essential. This project presents an IoT-based Air Quality Monitoring System designed to continuously track and analyze air quality parameters such as particulate matter (PM2.5 and PM10), carbon dioxide (CO2), carbon monoxide (CO), ozone (O3), and other pollutants.
The system employs a network of sensors to measure air quality parameters and a microcontroller to process the data. The data is transmitted via wireless communication protocols, such as Wi-Fi or LoRa, to a cloud-based platform for storage and analysis. Users can access real-time and historical data through a user-friendly mobile or web application, enabling them to make informed decisions and adopt precautionary measures.
The proposed system is scalable, cost-effective, and energy-efficient, making it suitable for urban and rural environments. It can be integrated with predictive analytics to identify pollution trends and notify users of hazardous conditions through automated alerts. By leveraging IoT technology, the system promotes awareness, aids in regulatory compliance, and supports initiatives aimed at improving air quality and public health.
This innovative solution demonstrates the potential of IoT in addressing global environmental challenges, paving the way for smarter, sustainable cities.
